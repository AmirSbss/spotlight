## 2. Message
On mute: yes. A café making espresso and iced lattes; follow @mahoor_hills. Every line the video wrote is in sources.md. Cut strips are clean: hard cuts, and no caption carries across a cut. The footage, though, adds an unsourced slogan ("coffee starts here") and a second coffee brand (CAFFINA). That breaks "no third-party logos" in s1, s7 and s8.

## 4. Verdict
As a café reel it's close: s2–s4 make you want the drink. As a public example, the hook's "CIMBALI coffee starts here" contradicts the plan, and a reviewer will see it first.

Top 6 changes:
1. Remove the third-party marks: a blur box over the fascia lettering in s1 and s8 and over the CAFFINA cup in s7, applied in source coordinates before the push. s1 and s8 are static, but s7's cup rides a moving tray, so track that box or reframe.
2. Drop s5 and give its 1 s to s4 or s3.
3. End the footage on a payoff: trim s8 to the look-up or close on the finished latte from s4.
4. Raise every caption about 180 px (last baseline at y ≤ 1340).
5. Reframe s6: push to about 1.15× anchored upper-centre, so the blurred glove leaves the frame and the drinks sit centre.
6. Sound: start the music at 46.25 so the strong hits land on the cuts (re-check them). If the clips have audio, put the espresso hiss under s2 and the pour and ice under s3, about 10 dB under the bed. Move the handle reveal and pop to 17.0. Add a 1.00→1.03 drift to the end card.
