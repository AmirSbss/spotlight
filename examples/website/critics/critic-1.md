- **1.5–3.0, man from behind at monitors, "to custom software."** Another label. The teal line over the pale dishdasha has weak contrast. Exits through a black frame (f90).
- **3.0–5.0, EN site.** The "Chat on WhatsApp" button shows a third-party glyph and a competing CTA for 3.5 s. The wave image and the empty "What we do" area fill about 60% of the frame. Body text and buttons are about 22 px, unreadable on a phone. The toggle tap at 4.5 s is a tiny ring.
- **5.0–6.5, AR site.** The same, mirrored. Non-Arabic readers get 1.5 s of text and no cue. Black frame on exit (f195).
- **6.5–9.0, "Set it up."** Clean. The exit is a grey haze, a fourth transition style.
- **9.0–11.5, "Run it."** Clean but generic.
- **11.5–14.0, "Grow it with you."** The man's face is cut off at the left edge. The green kicker over the pale sleeve and paper is the weakest caption. The 2.1x upscale looks soft.
- **14.0–17.0, rack, "A system you fully own and control."** Dark and muddy. Lowest motion in the video (frame difference about 0.025 for 2.5 s; not frozen per check.json), so it sags right before the CTA. Enters through a black frame (f420).
- **17.0–20.0, end card.** Fully in by 18.1 s and clear. The content uses only the middle third, and the tap ripple covers "a quote."

## 2. Message
On mute, yes. By 3.5 s the page headline (H1) says "BudTech, your technology partner in Oman and the GCC," and the end card gives "Request a quote" and budtech.om. The opener and kickers only label the picture. The three verbs add the partnership arc, and "own and control" adds a reason. Nothing invites the viewer before the end card. The hero sentence appears four times, so the web beat is repetition. Every on-screen claim is in sources.md, with no numbers or prices.

## 3. Sound
Measured: -14.1 LUFS, -2.2 dBTP.

**Hook (0–3.0 s).** No kick: flat bass under a low-passed bed, at -18 to -20 LUFS momentary against -13 after 6.5 s. The 1.5 s cut lands on nothing. The kick enters on the 3.0 cut and the highs open on "Set it up." Good landings, but late.

**Cuts.** A kick every 0.5 s from 3.0 to 16.5 s, and every cut from 3.0 to 14.0 lands on one. The kick drops out as the end card arrives at 17.0.

**Effects.** All seven sit on real transitions or taps, and none stings over the resolved end card. Each was measured in its own band against the same beat in music-only bars:
- Whoosh at 3.0: +26 dB, glaring.
- Click at 4.75: +14 dB.
- Whoosh at 6.5: about +10 dB.
- Whoosh at 17.0: +8 to +11 dB.
- Click at 18.5: +3 to +5 dB.
- Toggle at 5.0: about +1 dB, masked by the kick.
- Whoosh at 14.0: 0.0 dB, inaudible.

**End.** The music runs at full level to 19.4 s, then fades over 0.6 s. Correct.

## 4. Top 6 changes
1. **Hook audio:** put the kick under 0–3 s. Shift the track window, or cut the low-pass build to 1.5 s or less.
2. **Transitions:** replace the black dips at 3.0, 6.5 and 14.0, and the 8.75 haze, with hard cuts or a 4–6 frame push. Keep the whooshes.
3. **Web beat:** cut it from 3.5 s to about 2 s. A push-in crop on the EN H1 plus "Request a quote" removes the WhatsApp glyph, the wave image and the empty bottom. Then show the AR H1, tight, for 0.7 s, or drop it.
4. **Consulting shot:** reframe so the man's face is in frame, and darken the kicker scrim.
5. **Rack shot (14.0–17.0):** lift the exposure and add a real push-in, or trim to 2.2 s and give the time to the hook.
6. **Effect levels:** raise the 14.0 whoosh 4–6 dB and the 5.0 toggle about 2 dB. Lower the 3.0 whoosh at least 6 dB.
