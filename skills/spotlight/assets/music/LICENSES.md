# Music licences

Two kinds of track are bundled. All files were retrieved on 2026-10-12.

**Every track except `minimal-tech.mp3`** is by Sascha Ende, from ende.app. They are licensed CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0).
- **Credit:** ende.app's licence page says credit is optional, but each track page asks for it, so credit "Music by Sascha Ende at ende.app" in `sources.md`, and in `caption.txt` when there's room.
- **Content ID:** the licence page forbids registering the music with YouTube Content ID or similar systems.
- **AI:** several track pages name their producers as "Sascha and Marvin", and the site describes Marvin as Sascha's AI assistant.
- **The `upbeat` tracks 1, 9, 10, 11 and 12** are the same files the brag skill bundles (github.com/latent-spaces/brag at commit 8531ccb9f471ef5099abf930079e47b55c55bb21, `skills/brag/assets/music/`), byte for byte, as 192 kbps, 48 kHz MP3s.
- **`warm`, `calm`, `cinematic` and `upbeat-rock`** were downloaded from ende.app by the project's owner. Their audio is bit for bit the download: the stream was copied and the embedded cover art left out, and the decoded audio's MD5 matches. The rows give the SHA-256 of the original downloads.

**`minimal-tech.mp3`** is CC0 (Creative Commons Zero 1.0, a public-domain dedication). No attribution is required; the authors are listed as a courtesy.
- **Processing:** each was transcoded to 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 at 160 kbps CBR. Where the encoded true peak would have exceeded −1 dBTP, a single static gain of −0.5 to −1.2 dB was applied. There is no other processing (no normalisation, compression, trimming or EQ).

The licence quotes are verbatim from the source pages. For the CC0 tracks that came from Wikimedia Commons, the row also gives the original Free Music Archive (FMA) track page. That is where the artist published the track, and it states the licence in its own words.
